# Water Purification Methods

## Boiling (Most Reliable)
- Bring water to a rolling boil for **1 minute**
- At elevations above 6,500 ft: boil **3 minutes**

### Pros
- Kills bacteria, viruses, parasites

### Cons
- Does NOT remove chemicals or heavy metals

---

## Bleach (Emergency Use)
- Use **unscented household bleach (5–6%)**

### Dosage
- Clear water: 2 drops per quart (4 per liter)
- Cloudy water: double the dose

### Instructions
1. Mix well
2. Let sit for **30 minutes**
3. Should smell slightly like chlorine

---

## Filtration
- Use a **0.1–0.2 micron filter**

### Removes
- Bacteria
- Parasites

### Does NOT remove
- Viruses (unless combined with chemical treatment)

---

## Solar Disinfection (SODIS)
1. Use clear plastic bottles
2. Fill with water
3. Leave in direct sunlight for **6+ hours**

---

## Notes
- Always filter dirty water BEFORE chemical treatment
- When in doubt: **boil + filter**

## Priority
- In emergency: boil first, filter if possible
