# Water Storage

**Store at least 1 gallon per person per day — 2 weeks minimum.** Use the [Water Planner](/tools/water) to size it.

## Containers
- **Food-grade** plastic: HDPE (recycling **#2**), usually blue (blocks light and algae). Also PET (#1) soda/water bottles.
- **Avoid:** milk jugs (biodegrade and leak), containers that held chemicals, non-food-grade buckets.
- Glass works but breaks. Stainless steel is great but expensive.
- **Sizes:** mix of portable (1–7 gal) and bulk (55-gal drums, IBC totes). A full 55-gal drum weighs ~460 lb. Put it where it'll stay before filling.

## Sanitize Containers (CDC)
1. Wash with dish soap and water; rinse.
2. Mix **1 teaspoon unscented bleach per quart of water**.
3. Pour into the container, cap, shake so it touches every surface.
4. Wait **30 seconds**, pour out, air dry (or fill right away).

## Filling
- **Chlorinated city tap water:** fill and seal. No additives needed.
- **Well or untreated water:** add bleach per the [Bleach Dosing tool](/tools/bleach) (8 drops of 6% per gallon) before sealing.
- Use a **drinking-water-safe hose** (white/blue RV hose), not a garden hose.

## How Long It Lasts
- Self-filled, treated: **replace every 6 months** (FEMA). Sealed, treated water is often safe longer; if old, re-treat with bleach or boil before drinking.
- Commercial bottled water: follow the date. It's a quality date; the water doesn't "go bad" if sealed.
- Water preserver concentrates can extend storage up to 5 years.
- Label every container with the **fill date**.

## Where to Store
- **Cool, dark** place, away from gasoline, pesticides and chemicals (plastic is permeable to vapors).
- Off the floor on a pallet or wood: keeps it cooler and lets you inspect for leaks.
- Spread it around: some in the house, some in the garage, some in vehicles.
- Freezing: leave headspace in rigid containers.

## Hidden Water Already in Your House
| Source | Amount | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Water heater** | 30–80 gal | Turn off power/gas, close cold inlet, open a hot tap upstairs for air, drain from the bottom valve. First water may have sediment. Let settle |
| **Pipes** | A few gallons | Open the highest faucet, then drain from the lowest |
| **Toilet tank** (not bowl) | 1–3 gal each | OK if no chemical tablets/cleaners; treat before drinking |
| **Ice cubes / freezer** | Varies | Melt |
| **Canned fruits & vegetables** | Liquid | Drink the juice |
| **Bathtub (filled at first warning)** | 40–60 gal | Use a bathtub bladder (WaterBOB) to keep it clean |
| **Pool / hot tub** | Thousands | **Not for drinking** (chemicals) without serious treatment; good for flushing & washing |

## Shut the Main Valve
If there's a water main break, flood, or contamination warning, **close your home's main water valve** to keep contaminated water out of your pipes and water heater. Know where it is now.

## Priority
- **Fill everything at the first warning. Then ration drinking water first.**
