# Allergic Reactions & Anaphylaxis

## Recognize Anaphylaxis
A severe, whole-body allergic reaction, usually within minutes of exposure (stings, foods like nuts or shellfish, medications). Suspect it when **two or more body systems** are involved, or **breathing or blood pressure** are affected:
- **Skin:** hives, flushing, swelling of lips, tongue, face
- **Airway:** throat tightness, hoarse voice, wheezing, trouble breathing
- **Circulation:** dizziness, fainting, weak fast pulse, pale
- **Gut:** vomiting, cramps, diarrhea
- A sense of doom

## Treatment
1. **Epinephrine auto-injector (EpiPen, Auvi-Q, etc.) — immediately.**
   - **Outer thigh**, through clothing if needed. Hold for the time on the label (usually 3–10 s).
   - **Don't wait** to see if it gets better. Delayed epinephrine is the main cause of death.
2. Call for help if any exists.
3. **Position:** lie flat with legs raised. Sit up if breathing is hard. Side position if vomiting. **Don't let them stand or walk suddenly.**
4. **Second dose** after **5–15 minutes** if there's no improvement or symptoms return.
5. **Antihistamine** (diphenhydramine/Benadryl 25–50 mg adults) helps itching and hives but **does not replace epinephrine**.
6. If they have a **rescue inhaler** for asthma, use it for wheezing *in addition to* epinephrine.
7. Watch for at least **4–6 hours**. A second wave (biphasic reaction) can occur.
8. Not breathing → [CPR](/medical/cpr_adult).

## Mild Allergic Reactions
Localized hives, itching, sneezing, no breathing or circulation problems:
- Antihistamine (diphenhydramine, cetirizine, loratadine)
- Cool compresses; hydrocortisone cream for skin
- Watch for progression

## Stings
- Scrape the stinger out quickly with a fingernail or card.
- Cold pack, antihistamine.

## Preparedness
- Anyone with known severe allergies should have **at least 2 auto-injectors** and keep one on them.
- Epinephrine degrades past its expiration date, but in a true emergency an expired pen is **better than none**.
- Store at room temperature, away from heat and freezing.

## Priority
- **Epinephrine early. Outer thigh. Second dose in 5–15 minutes if needed.**
