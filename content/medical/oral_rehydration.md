# Oral Rehydration Solution & Diarrhea

Diarrheal disease from contaminated water is one of the **biggest killers after disasters**, especially of children. Oral rehydration solution (ORS) is cheap, simple, and life-saving.

## Home ORS Recipe (WHO/UNICEF)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| **Clean water** (boiled & cooled or disinfected) | **1 liter** (about 1 quart) |
| **Sugar** | **6 level teaspoons** |
| **Salt** | **½ level teaspoon** |

- Stir until dissolved. **Measure carefully.** Too much salt is dangerous, especially for children. It should taste **no saltier than tears**.
- Optional: ½ cup orange juice or a mashed banana adds potassium.
- Make fresh daily. Discard after 24 hours.
- Commercial ORS packets are better if you have them; store some.

## How Much to Give

| Age | After each loose stool | Daily guide |
|---|---|---|
| Under 2 years | ¼–½ cup (50–100 mL) | Small sips by spoon every 1–2 min |
| 2–10 years | ½–1 cup (100–200 mL) | |
| Older children & adults | As much as wanted | 2–3+ liters/day |

**If vomiting:** wait 10 minutes, then continue with smaller, slower sips. Most fluid still gets absorbed.

## Keep Feeding
- **Continue breastfeeding** throughout.
- Eat normally as soon as possible: rice, potatoes, bananas, bread, soups.
- **Zinc** supplements (10–20 mg/day for 10–14 days) reduce duration in children, per WHO.

## Signs of Dehydration

| Mild–moderate | **Severe — urgent** |
|---|---|
| Thirsty, restless | Lethargic, floppy, unconscious |
| Dry mouth, few tears | Sunken eyes, no tears |
| Dark, less urine | **No urine for 8+ hours** |
| | Skin pinch goes back **very slowly** (> 2 s) |
| | Unable to drink |

## Danger Signs — Need Advanced Care
- **Blood in stool** (dysentery) with fever: may need antibiotics
- High fever, stiff neck, severe abdominal pain
- Diarrhea lasting > 14 days
- Profuse "rice-water" stool (cholera): massive fluid replacement needed
- Infants under 6 months with significant diarrhea

## Prevention
- [Purify water](/water/water_purification_methods). When in doubt, boil.
- **Wash hands with soap** after the toilet and before food. This cuts diarrhea by around 30–40%.
- Keep latrines **away from water sources** (see [Sanitation](/homestead/sanitation_hygiene)).
- Cover food from flies; cook food thoroughly.

## Priority
- **1 liter water + 6 teaspoons sugar + ½ teaspoon salt. Keep drinking.**
