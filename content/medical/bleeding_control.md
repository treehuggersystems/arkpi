# Bleeding Control Basics

## Step 1: Direct Pressure
- Apply firm pressure with cloth or bandage
- Maintain pressure for several minutes

---

## Step 2: Elevate (if possible)
- Raise wound above heart level

---

## Step 3: Pack the Wound
- Use gauze or cloth
- Pack deeply into wound
- Continue pressure

---

## Step 4: Tourniquet (Severe Bleeding Only)
- Place **2–3 inches above wound**
- Tighten until bleeding stops
- Note the time

---

## Signs of Severe Bleeding
- Blood spurting or pooling
- Soaking through bandages quickly
- Weakness, dizziness

---

## DO NOT
- Remove soaked bandages (add more instead)
- Loosen a tourniquet once applied

---

## Priority
- Stop bleeding FIRST, everything else second
