# Mass-Casualty Triage (START)

When there are more injured people than helpers, triage finds the people who will die **without immediate help but will live with it**. Spend **under 60 seconds** per person. Only fix airway and massive bleeding during triage.

## The Algorithm

1. **"Everyone who can walk, come to me."** → **GREEN** (Minor)
2. For each person remaining, check **breathing**:
   - Not breathing → **reposition airway** once.
     - Still not breathing → **BLACK** (Expectant / deceased)
     - Starts breathing → **RED**
   - Breathing **> 30 per minute** → **RED**
   - Breathing < 30 → go on
3. Check **perfusion**:
   - **No radial (wrist) pulse**, or capillary refill > 2 seconds → **RED**. Control bleeding.
   - Radial pulse present → go on
4. Check **mental status**:
   - **Can't follow simple commands** ("squeeze my hand") → **RED**
   - Follows commands → **YELLOW**

**Remember: RPM — 30, 2, Can do.** Respirations 30 · Perfusion 2 seconds · Mental status "can do".

## Categories

| Tag | Priority | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **RED** — Immediate | Life-threatening, survivable with fast care | Severe bleeding controlled by tourniquet, shock, airway problems |
| **YELLOW** — Delayed | Serious but stable for hours | Fractures, moderate burns, wounds without shock |
| **GREEN** — Minor | Walking wounded | Cuts, bruises, sprains |
| **BLACK** — Expectant | Dead or unsurvivable with available resources | Not breathing after airway repositioned |

## JumpSTART (Children ~1–8 yrs)
- Not breathing with a pulse → give **5 rescue breaths**. Breathes → RED; doesn't → BLACK.
- Breathing < 15 or > 45 per minute → RED.
- Mental status: **AVPU** — "P" with inappropriate response or "U" → RED.

## Practical Tips
- Mark people: colored tape, marker on forehead, or tags tied to a wrist.
- **Re-triage** often — people change categories.
- Use GREEN patients as helpers: holding pressure, carrying, watching others.
- Log names, tags and times in the [Logbook](/tools/logbook).

## Priority
- **Do the most good for the most people. Keep moving.**
