# Heat Exhaustion & Heat Stroke

Without air conditioning, summer heat becomes a major threat to the elderly, small children, people with chronic illness, and anyone working hard.

## Compare

| | Heat exhaustion | **Heat stroke — EMERGENCY** |
|---|---|---|
| Mental state | Normal or mildly anxious | **Confused, slurred speech, combative, seizures, unconscious** |
| Skin | Cool, pale, **sweating heavily** | **Hot**, red; may be dry *or* sweating |
| Temp | Normal to 104°F | **Above 104°F (40°C)** |
| Other | Headache, nausea, dizziness, cramps, weakness, fast pulse | Fast strong pulse, rapid breathing |

**Any confusion in the heat = treat as heat stroke.**

## Heat Exhaustion — Treatment
1. Move to **shade/cool area**, lie down, raise legs.
2. Loosen/remove extra clothing.
3. Cool with wet cloths, fanning, cool water on skin.
4. **Drink** cool water or [oral rehydration solution](/medical/oral_rehydration) in sips.
5. Rest for the remainder of the day. If not improving within 30–60 min, or confusion develops → treat as heat stroke.

## Heat Stroke — Cool First, Cool Fast
Every minute counts. Brain damage and death happen quickly.
1. **Cold water immersion** is best: tub, stream, livestock trough, tarp "taco" filled with ice water, neck-deep. Keep the head supported and above water.
2. No immersion? **Soak and fan:** pour cold water over the whole body continuously while fanning hard. Ice packs on **neck, armpits, groin**, and cheeks/palms/soles.
3. Continue until they're alert/talking normally or temperature ~102°F (39°C), then stop to avoid overcooling.
4. Nothing by mouth if confused. Put on side if vomiting.
5. Monitor — they can relapse.

## Heat Cramps
Painful muscle spasms during work. Rest, stretch gently, drink electrolyte fluids.

## Prevention Without Power
- Work at **dawn and dusk**; rest 11 AM–4 PM.
- Drink **before** you're thirsty: about 1 qt/hr of hard work in heat, with electrolytes. Don't exceed ~1.5 qt/hr.
- Light, loose, light-colored clothing; wide-brim hat; wet bandana on neck.
- Sleep in the lowest, coolest room; basements stay cool.
- Open windows at night, close windows and blinds by day.
- Check on vulnerable neighbors twice a day.
- Check the [Heat Index tool](/tools/weather).

## Priority
- **Confused + hot = heat stroke. Cold water immersion immediately.**
