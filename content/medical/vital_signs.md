# Vital Signs & Patient Assessment

## Normal Vital Signs (at rest)

| | Adult | Child (1–10 yrs) | Infant (< 1 yr) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heart rate | **60–100**/min | 70–130 | 100–160 |
| Breathing | **12–20**/min | 20–30 | 30–60 |
| Blood pressure | ~**120/80** (systolic 90–140) | Systolic ≈ 90 + 2 × age | — |
| Temperature | **97–99.5°F** (36.1–37.5°C) | Same | Same; ≥ 100.4°F in < 3 months = serious |
| SpO₂ (pulse ox) | **95–100%** | 95–100% | 95–100% |
| Capillary refill | **< 2 s** | < 2 s | < 2 s |

## Taking Vitals Without Equipment
- **Pulse:** two fingers on the wrist (radial) or neck (carotid). Count 30 s × 2.
- **Breathing:** watch the chest rise without telling them (people change their breathing when watched). Count 30 s × 2.
- **Rough blood pressure:** a **palpable wrist pulse** usually means systolic is around 80 or more. No wrist pulse but a neck pulse means it's low — **shock**.
- **Capillary refill:** press a fingernail 5 s, release, count seconds for color to return.
- **Temperature:** back of hand on forehead vs. your own. Note "hot / normal / cold / clammy."

**Record vitals with times every 5–15 minutes.** Trends matter more than single numbers. Use the [Logbook](/tools/logbook).

## Primary Survey — ABCDE (find killers fast)
- **(X) eXsanguination** — massive bleeding → [tourniquet / pressure](/medical/bleeding_control)
- **A**irway — open? noisy? (head-tilt chin-lift / jaw thrust)
- **B**reathing — rate, effort, both sides moving, wounds to chest
- **C**irculation — pulse, skin, capillary refill, other bleeding
- **D**isability — level of consciousness: **AVPU**
  - **A**lert · responds to **V**oice · responds to **P**ain · **U**nresponsive
- **E**xposure — check whole body for injuries, then **prevent hypothermia**

## History — SAMPLE
**S**igns/symptoms · **A**llergies · **M**edications · **P**ast medical history · **L**ast oral intake · **E**vents leading up

## Pain — OPQRST
**O**nset · **P**rovokes/palliates · **Q**uality (sharp, dull, burning) · **R**adiates · **S**everity (0–10) · **T**ime

## Red Flags
- Breathing < 8 or > 30
- Heart rate < 50 or > 130 (adult)
- No radial pulse
- Decreasing level of consciousness
- SpO₂ < 90%
- Chest pain with sweating, nausea, or pain into arm/jaw: possible **heart attack** (chew 325 mg aspirin if not allergic)
- Sudden face droop, arm weakness, speech difficulty: possible **stroke**. Note the time.
