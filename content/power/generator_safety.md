# Generator Safety & Use

## ⚠ The Two Killers

### 1. Carbon Monoxide
- **Outdoors only**, at least **20 ft from the house**, exhaust pointed away from doors, windows, vents.
- **Never** in a garage (even with the door open), basement, crawlspace, porch, or shed.
- Battery-powered **CO alarms** on every level and outside sleeping areas.
- Symptoms: headache, dizziness, nausea, confusion, "flu without fever." **Everyone in the house feeling sick at once = get out into fresh air now.**

### 2. Backfeeding
- **Never plug a generator into a wall outlet or dryer outlet** ("suicide cord"). It sends 240 V back into the utility lines and can **electrocute line workers** and neighbors, and burn your house down when power returns.
- Use a **transfer switch** or a **breaker interlock kit** with a properly rated inlet, installed per code. Or run extension cords directly to appliances.

## Sizing
| Load | Running watts | Starting surge |
|---|---|---|
| Refrigerator | 150–800 | 1,200–2,200 |
| Chest freezer | 100–400 | 500–1,500 |
| Well pump (½–1 hp) | 750–1,500 | 2,000–4,000 |
| Sump pump (½ hp) | 800–1,000 | 1,500–2,500 |
| Furnace blower (gas furnace) | 400–800 | 1,000–2,000 |
| Window AC (10k BTU) | 900–1,200 | 2,000–3,000 |
| Microwave | 1,000–1,500 | — |
| Space heater | 1,500 | — |
| Lights, TV, internet, chargers | 200–500 | — |

Add up running watts, then add the **largest single surge**. Start big motor loads one at a time. Use the [Generator Fuel calculator](/tools/generator).

## Extension Cords
| Load (at 120 V) | Up to 50 ft | Up to 100 ft |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 10 A (1,200 W) | 16 AWG | 14 AWG |
| 10–15 A (1,800 W) | 14 AWG | 12 AWG |
| 15–20 A (2,400 W) | 12 AWG | 10 AWG |
Use outdoor-rated, grounded (3-prong) cords. Never run cords under rugs. Inspect for damage.

## Fuel Storage
- **Gasoline:** approved containers, **outdoors** or in a detached shed, away from ignition sources.
  - Add **stabilizer** (Sta-Bil, PRI-G) when filled: lasts ~1–2 years vs 3–6 months untreated.
  - **Ethanol-free** gas stores better and is gentler on small engines.
  - Rotate into vehicles every 6–12 months.
- **Propane:** doesn't degrade. Store tanks **upright, outdoors**, never in a garage or basement.
- **Diesel:** 1 year+ with treatment (biocide + stabilizer).
- Refuel only when the generator is **off and cool**.

## Maintenance
- **Run it monthly** under load for 15–30 min.
- Change oil after the first 5–20 hrs (break-in), then every **50–100 hrs** (check manual). **During a long outage that's every 4–8 days of continuous use.** Stock oil and filters.
- Keep spare **spark plug, air filter, oil**, and a fuel filter.
- Storing over 30 days: run dry or use stabilized fuel, or shut fuel valve and run the carb empty.
- See [Small Engine Troubleshooting](/repair/small_engine_troubleshooting).

## Weather
- Keep it **dry**: use a generator tent/canopy designed for it with open sides. Don't run it in standing water.
- Chain and lock it. Generators are stolen during outages.
- They're **loud**: hearing protection, and consider noise/security implications.

## Priority
- **Outside, 20 feet, CO alarms inside, never backfeed.**
