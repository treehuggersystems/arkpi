# Banana Nut Bread with Pecans

## Ingredients

**Wet**
- 227 g unsalted butter, softened
- 200 g granulated sugar
- 2 eggs (about 100 g), beaten
- 5 g vanilla extract (1 tsp)
- 240 g ripe bananas (about 2 medium-large)

**Dry**
- 250 g all-purpose flour
- 5 g baking soda (1 tsp)
- 3 g salt (½ tsp)

**Pecans**
- 75 g toasted chopped pecans, divided: 50 g for the batter, 25 g for the top

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 175°C / 350°F. Grease a 9×5 in loaf pan and line the bottom with parchment.
2. Cream the butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the eggs and vanilla.
4. Add the bananas and beat until combined.
5. By hand, fold in the flour, baking soda, and salt. Don't use a mixer for this step, or the loaf will be tough.
6. Fold in 50 g of the pecans.
7. Spread the batter in the pan and sprinkle the remaining 25 g of pecans on top.
8. Bake 60–70 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean. Start checking at 50 minutes. If the top browns too fast, cover loosely with foil.
9. Cool 10 minutes in the pan, then turn out onto a rack to cool completely.

## Notes
- Use very ripe bananas, with lots of brown spots and no green.
- Weights for flour and pecans assume spooned-and-leveled flour and chopped pecans. If you scoop flour straight from the bag, weigh it.
- Walnuts or chocolate chips can replace the pecans.
