# Peach Cobbler (2-2-2 Cast Iron Skillet)

## Ingredients
- 2 cups (240 g) all-purpose flour  
- 2 cups (400 g) sugar  
- 2 cups (480 g) milk  
- 1 stick (1/2 cup / 113 g) butter  
- 4 cups diced peaches (16 oz / ~450 g), drained  

---

## Instructions

### 1. Preheat
- Preheat oven to **350°F**
- Place a **cast iron skillet** in the oven to heat

### 2. Melt Butter
- Add butter to the hot skillet  
- Let it melt completely and get hot

### 3. Make Batter (2-2-2)
- Mix flour, sugar, and milk until smooth

### 4. Assemble (Do Not Stir)
- Pour batter over hot melted butter  
- Ladle drained peaches evenly over the top  
- Do **not** stir

### 5. Bake
- Bake at **350°F until top is golden and crispy**
- Approx. **40–50 minutes**

---

## Notes
- Batter will rise around the peaches as it bakes  
- Draining peaches prevents excess moisture  
- Cast iron helps create crispy edges and better browning  
- Look for bubbling edges and a golden, crisp top as doneness cues  
- Let rest briefly before serving
