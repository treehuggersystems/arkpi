# Simple Soft White Sandwich Bread (Cast Iron Loaf Pan)

A reliable, fluffy everyday sandwich bread recipe that works great in a cast iron loaf pan. Soft crumb, good structure, and a buttery top without being overly complicated.

## Ingredients
- 500g bread flour (or all-purpose flour)
- 325g warm milk (105°F–110°F / 40°C–43°C)
- 35g sugar or honey
- 5g salt
- 7g instant yeast
- 42g salted butter, softened

### Optional Additions
- 1 egg (for richer bread)
- Melted butter for brushing after baking

## Equipment
- Cast iron loaf pan (9x5 preferred)
- Stand mixer or large mixing bowl
- Kitchen scale

## Instructions

### 1. Mix
- Add warm milk, sugar or honey, and yeast to bowl  
- Let sit for 2–3 minutes  

Add:
- Flour
- Salt
- Butter
- Egg (optional)

- Mix until a shaggy dough forms  

### 2. Knead
- Knead dough:
  - 8–10 minutes with mixer
  - 10–12 minutes by hand

Dough should become:
- Smooth
- Soft
- Slightly tacky
- Elastic

If too sticky:
- Add flour 1 tablespoon at a time

- Do **not** make the dough dry  

### 3. First Rise
- Place dough into lightly greased bowl  
- Cover and let rise 60–90 minutes until doubled in size  

### 4. Shape
- Lightly flour counter  
- Flatten dough gently into rectangle  
- Roll tightly into loaf shape  

Make sure to:
- Pinch seams closed
- Tuck ends slightly

- Place seam-side down into greased cast iron loaf pan  

### 5. Second Rise
- Cover loosely  
- Let rise 35–60 minutes  

- Dough should rise about 1 inch above pan rim  

Meanwhile:
- Preheat oven to **375°F**

### 6. Bake
- Bake for **35–40 minutes**

Top should become:
- Deep golden brown

Internal temperature should reach:
- **190°F–200°F**

If top browns too quickly:
- Loosely tent with foil during final portion of bake  

### 7. Finish
- Immediately brush top with melted butter  
- Rest 10 minutes in pan  
- Remove to cooling rack  

- Try not to slice for at least 30 minutes  

## Notes

### Cast Iron Tip
Cast iron holds heat aggressively, helping create:
- Better oven spring
- Softer interior
- Nicely browned crust

If loaf gets too dark on bottom:
- Place sheet pan underneath loaf pan during bake  

### Storage
- Stays soft about 3 days in bread bag/container
- Freezes very well sliced

### Extra Soft Bread Option
Replace:
- 25g flour

With:
- 25g instant potato flakes

This creates an extremely soft sandwich bread.

### Better Flavor Option
After first rise:
- Cover dough
- Refrigerate overnight

Next day:
- Shape
- Rise
- Bake

This dramatically improves flavor.
