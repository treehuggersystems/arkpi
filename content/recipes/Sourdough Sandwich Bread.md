# Easy Sourdough Sandwich Bread (Distilled)

## Ingredients
- 65g active sourdough starter (fed and bubbly)
- 300g lukewarm water
- 400g bread flour
- 100g all-purpose flour
- 12g salt
- 14g sugar
- 15g oil (olive or avocado)

## Instructions

### 1. Prep Starter
- Feed starter 4–8 hours before baking  
- Use when active and bubbly  

### 2. Mix Dough
- Combine water and starter and mix well  

Add:
- Bread flour
- All-purpose flour
- Salt
- Sugar
- Oil

- Mix until no dry flour remains and a shaggy dough forms  

### 3. Rest (Autolyse)
- Cover dough  
- Rest for **1 hour**  

### 4. Bulk Fermentation (First Rise)
- Shape dough into a ball  
- Cover and let rise until doubled in size  

Estimated time:
- **8–10 hours** depending on room temperature  

### 5. Shape
- Turn dough onto lightly floured surface  
- Gently flatten to remove large air pockets  
- Fold sides inward to form rectangle  

- Rest dough for **10 minutes**  

- Tighten final shape  
- Place into lightly oiled loaf pan  

### 6. Second Rise (Proof)
- Cover dough  
- Let rise until dough reaches about **1/2 inch above pan rim**  

Estimated time:
- **2–4 hours** depending on temperature  

### 7. Bake
- Preheat oven to **400°F (204°C)**  
- Bake for **40 minutes**  

Top should become:
- Golden brown  

### 8. Cool
- Cool in pan for **5 minutes**  
- Transfer to cooling rack  
- Allow loaf to cool completely before slicing  

## Notes
- No scoring needed  
- Rise times depend heavily on room temperature  
- Oil, sugar, and mixed flours help create a softer texture
