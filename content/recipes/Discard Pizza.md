# Easy Sourdough Pizza Crust

Adapted from:
https://www.farmhouseonboone.com/how-to-make-easy-sourdough-pizza-crust/

## Ingredients
- 240g sourdough starter or discard
- 14g olive oil, plus more for the pan
- 3g salt
- 1g Italian seasoning (optional)

## Equipment
- Cast iron skillet or pizza stone
- Oven

## Instructions

### 1. Preheat Oven
- Preheat oven to **425°F (220°C)**  
- Place cast iron skillet or pizza stone inside while preheating  

### 2. Prepare Pan
- Carefully remove hot skillet or pizza stone  
- Drizzle surface with olive oil  

### 3. Add Dough
- Pour sourdough starter/discard into hot pan  
- Spread into a thin, even layer  

### 4. Season
- Drizzle with a little more olive oil  
- Sprinkle with salt and Italian seasoning  

### 5. Pre-Bake Crust
- Bake about **10 minutes**  
- Crust should begin firming up and pulling away from edges  

### 6. Add Toppings
- Remove from oven  
- Add sauce, cheese, and desired toppings  

### 7. Finish Baking
- Return to oven  
- Bake another **8–10 minutes** until cheese is melted and bubbly  

## Notes
- Thin layers create a crispier crust  
- Works especially well with sourdough discard  
- Fresh tomato toppings pair very well  
- Crust can be frozen after first bake for future use
