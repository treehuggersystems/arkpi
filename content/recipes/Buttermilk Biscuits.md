# Buttermilk Biscuits (Best Batch So Far)

## Ingredients
- 360g all-purpose flour
- 6g salt
- 18g baking powder
- 113g cold unsalted butter, grated
- 50g beef tallow, slightly warmed
- 300g 2% milk
- 19g white vinegar

## Equipment
- Large mixing bowl
- Sifter
- Box grater
- Cutting board
- 12-inch cast iron skillet

## Instructions

### 1. Preheat Oven
- Preheat oven to **500°F**

### 2. Make Buttermilk Substitute
- Combine milk and vinegar  
- Let rest for at least **10 minutes**  

### 3. Mix Dry Ingredients
- In large bowl, whisk together:
  - Flour
  - Salt
  - Baking powder

### 4. Sift Dry Mixture
- Run mixture through sifter to:
  - Remove clumps
  - Fully combine ingredients

### 5. Prepare Fats
- Slightly warm beef tallow until soft and malleable  
- Grate cold butter directly into flour mixture  

### 6. Cut in Butter & Tallow
- Incorporate butter and tallow until mixture resembles coarse crumbs  

### 7. Add Liquid
- Pour in milk mixture  
- Stir until just combined  

### 8. Shape Dough
- Turn dough onto lightly floured cutting board  
- Gently press into shape  

- Do **not** knead dough  

### 9. Cut Biscuits
- Cut biscuits to desired size  
- Place into **12-inch cast iron skillet**  

### 10. Bake
- Bake at **500°F** for about **10 minutes**  

Biscuits should become:
- Golden brown on top

## Notes
- Milk and vinegar worked well as a buttermilk substitute even without heavy curdling  
- Beef tallow added excellent flavor and texture  
- Slightly warming the tallow made incorporation easier  
- Grated cold butter distributed evenly through dough  
- Sifting improved texture and consistency  
- Pressing dough gently instead of kneading helped keep biscuits tender  
- This batch produced the best results so far
