# Sourdough Hamburger Buns

## Yield
- 8 buns (~90–100g each)

## Ingredients
- 100g active sourdough starter (fed and bubbly)
- 240g warm milk
- 1 large egg (~50g)
- 40g sugar
- 10g salt
- 60g softened butter
- 500g bread flour

## Instructions

### 1. Mix Dough
- In a bowl, combine:
  - Warm milk
  - Sourdough starter
  - Egg
  - Sugar

- Mix well  

Add:
- Bread flour
- Salt

- Mix until shaggy dough forms  
- Add softened butter  
- Knead until smooth and elastic  

Kneading time:
- About **8–10 minutes**

### 2. Bulk Fermentation (First Rise)
- Cover dough  
- Let rise until doubled in size  

Estimated time:
- **6–10 hours** depending on room temperature  

### 3. Divide & Shape
- Turn dough onto work surface  
- Divide into **8 equal pieces (~95g each)**  

For each bun:
- Pull edges inward
- Flip seam-side down
- Roll into tight ball to create surface tension  

### 4. Bench Rest
- Let dough balls rest for **10–15 minutes**  

### 5. Final Shape
- Tighten each ball again if needed  
- Place onto parchment-lined baking sheet  
- Space evenly apart  

### 6. Proof (Second Rise)
- Cover loosely  
- Let rise until:
  - Puffy
  - Slightly springy to touch  

Estimated time:
- **2–4 hours**

### 7. Egg Wash (Optional)
- Mix:
  - 1 egg
  - Splash of water

- Brush tops lightly  

Optional topping:
- Sesame seeds  

### 8. Bake
- Preheat oven to **375°F (190°C)**  
- Bake for **18–25 minutes**  

Buns should become:
- Golden brown on top

Optional internal temp:
- Around **200°F**

### 9. Finish Soft Tops
- Brush tops with melted butter immediately after baking  

### 10. Cool
- Cool on wire rack before slicing  

## Notes
- Milk, butter, and egg create soft enriched buns  
- Avoid overproofing for lighter buns  
- Tight shaping improves rise and structure  
- Cover buns with towel while cooling for extra softness
