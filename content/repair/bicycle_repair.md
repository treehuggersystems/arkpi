# Bicycle Repair

No fuel, no electronics, 10–15 mph with cargo, and fixable with hand tools: the bicycle is the best grid-down transportation.

## Fixing a Flat
1. Shift to the smallest rear cog. Open brake quick-release (rim brakes), open wheel quick-release or loosen axle nuts, remove wheel.
2. Deflate fully. Hook **tire levers** under the bead, lever one side of the tire off the rim.
3. Pull the tube out. **Find the cause:** run fingers carefully inside the tire for thorns/glass/wire. Check rim tape covers all spoke holes.
4. Find the hole: inflate the tube, listen/feel, or submerge in water.
5. **Patch:** rough up the area with sandpaper, thin layer of glue, **let it dry until tacky (~5 min)**, press patch firmly. Or use a new tube.
6. Put a little air in the tube, insert valve first, tuck tube into tire, push the bead back on with thumbs (avoid levers that pinch the tube).
7. Inflate to the pressure on the tire sidewall. Check bead seated evenly.

**Valves:** Schrader (car-style) or Presta (thin, unscrew the tip nut to inflate). Adapters let a car pump fill Presta.

## Brakes
- **Rim brakes:** pads should hit the rim squarely, not the tire. Barrel adjuster on the lever/caliper tightens cable (turn out = tighter). Replace pads when grooves are gone.
- **Mechanical disc:** adjust pad knobs/cable; keep oil off rotors and pads (contaminated pads squeal and won't stop: replace).
- **Hydraulic disc:** bleeding needs a kit and the right fluid (DOT or mineral oil, never mixed). Mechanical brakes are easier to maintain long-term.

## Gears (Derailleur)
- **Chain won't shift to bigger cog:** turn the rear **barrel adjuster counter-clockwise** ¼ turn at a time.
- **Won't shift to smaller cog / noisy:** turn barrel adjuster clockwise.
- **Chain falls off the ends:** set the H/L limit screws.
- Bent derailleur hanger causes endless shifting trouble. Carry a spare hanger for your frame.

## Chain
- **Clean and lube** regularly: wipe, apply one drop per roller, wipe excess. Dirty dry chains wear out fast.
- **Stretched chain** (0.75% with a chain checker) wears cogs. Replace it.
- **Broken chain:** remove the damaged link with a **chain tool** and rejoin with a **quick link** (match your speed: 8, 9, 10, 11-speed).
- Single-speed / internally geared hubs need far less maintenance.

## Spares & Tools for the Long Haul
- Tubes ×4, patch kit ×2, **tire boots** (or dollar bill/Tyvek for sidewall cuts), 2 spare tires
- Tire levers, pump with gauge, multi-tool with chain breaker, spoke wrench
- Quick links, spare chain, brake pads, brake & shift cables + housing
- Chain lube, grease, rags
- Cargo: rear rack, panniers, a trailer (100+ lb capacity)

## Long-Term Bike Choice
Steel frame (weldable), rim or mechanical disc brakes, wide tires (2"+), common wheel sizes (26"/700c/29"), simple drivetrain. Avoid proprietary e-bike parts unless you have solar charging.
