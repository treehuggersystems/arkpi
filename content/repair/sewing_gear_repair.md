# Sewing & Gear Repair

## Kit
Needles (assorted, plus **heavy upholstery/sailmaker needles** and a curved needle) · **polyester thread** (heavy-duty, plus upholstery/bonded nylon for gear) · thimble · seam ripper · small scissors · safety pins · buttons · patches (denim, nylon ripstop, Tenacious Tape) · seam sealer · Shoe Goo / Aquaseal · zipper pulls · cordage · awl (Speedy Stitcher sewing awl)

## Essential Hand Stitches
| Stitch | How | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Running stitch** | In-and-out in a line, even spacing | Quick seams, basting |
| **Backstitch** | Come up one stitch ahead, go back down at the end of the last stitch | **Strongest hand seam.** Replaces machine stitching |
| **Whip / overcast** | Loop over the fabric edge repeatedly | Edges, closing tears, preventing fraying |
| **Ladder (slip) stitch** | Alternating small stitches inside each fold, pull tight | Invisible closure of a split seam |
| **Blanket stitch** | Loop each stitch through the previous loop along an edge | Edges, patches on wool |

**Always knot** at start (double knot or a few tiny stitches in place) and finish with 3 small stitches over each other.

## Common Fixes
- **Split seam:** turn inside out, backstitch along the original seam line, ¼ in past each end of the split.
- **Hole / tear:** trim loose threads, put a patch on the **inside** (or outside for heavy wear), whip-stitch the edges; or darn small holes in socks (weave thread across and then through).
- **Button:** sew through the holes 6–8 times over a pin (spacer), remove the pin, wrap thread around the shank 5–6 times, knot.
- **Zipper won't close (teeth separate):** the slider is worn. Squeeze the back of the slider gently with pliers on both sides. Missing pull: use a key ring or paracord.
- **Stuck zipper:** rub pencil graphite, soap, or wax on the teeth.

## Outdoor Gear
- **Tent / tarp / rain jacket tear:** clean with alcohol, apply **Tenacious Tape** (round the corners) on both sides; seal seams with seam sealer.
- **Leaky tent seam:** seam sealer on the inside of the seam, let cure 24 hrs.
- **Broken pack strap / buckle:** sew webbing with a **box-X stitch** using heavy thread and an awl. Carry spare side-release buckles.
- **Sleeping pad leak:** find with soapy water, patch kit or Seam Grip.
- **Duct tape** holds almost anything temporarily. Wrap some around a water bottle or trekking pole.

## Boots
- **Sole separating:** clean and dry both surfaces, rough them up, apply **Shoe Goo / Barge cement**, clamp or wrap tightly 24 hrs.
- Cracked leather: clean, condition with oil/wax (mink oil, Sno-Seal). Waterproof seams with wax.
- Broken laces: paracord inner strands or full paracord with ends melted.

## Waterproofing
- Waxed canvas: rub in wax (beeswax + paraffin), heat with a hair dryer to melt in.
- DWR jackets: wash with tech wash, tumble dry/iron on low to reactivate, re-spray if needed.

## Making Do
- Old clothing → patches, rags, bandages, cordage (twist strips), quilts.
- Paracord: 7 inner strands are thread for heavy sewing and fishing line.
- Learn to **knit or crochet** socks and hats. Wool is warm when wet.
