# Cooking Without Power

## ⚠ Carbon Monoxide
**Never** use charcoal grills, camp stoves, propane grills, generators or turkey fryers **indoors, in a garage, or in a tent.** CO is odorless and kills in your sleep. Run a battery CO alarm. Don't use a gas oven to heat the house.

## Options Ranked by Fuel Efficiency
| Method | Fuel | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Retained-heat (haybox) cooker** | Saves 50–70% fuel | Beans, rice, stews, grains |
| **Solar oven** | Free (sunny days) | Slow cooking, baking, pasteurizing water |
| **Rocket stove** | Twigs and sticks | Boiling, frying. Very efficient wood burn |
| **Wood stove top** | Firewood (already heating home) | Everything in winter |
| Propane camp stove / grill (outdoors) | Propane | Fast, controllable |
| Charcoal + Dutch oven (outdoors) | Charcoal | Baking, braising |
| Open campfire | Lots of wood | Least efficient |

## Retained-Heat (Haybox / Wonderbag) Cooking
1. Bring food to a **full boil** for a few minutes (beans: 10–15 min rolling boil).
2. Lid on, move the whole pot into an **insulated box**: cooler stuffed with blankets, pillows, straw, or a sleeping bag.
3. Leave it to cook for **2–3× the normal time**. Rice ~1–1.5 hrs, beans 4–8 hrs (after soaking).
4. Re-boil before eating if it has cooled below **140°F** for food safety.

## Rocket Stove
- An insulated L-shaped chimney: fuel goes in the side, burns hot and clean, flame goes straight up to the pot.
- Improvised: **#10 can + 4 soup cans**, or **16 concrete blocks** stacked in an L.
- Burns small sticks efficiently with little smoke.

## Solar Oven
- Box cooker: insulated box, black interior, glass/plastic lid, foil reflectors. Reaches **250–350°F** in full sun.
- Use dark, thin pots with lids. Cook between 10 AM–2 PM. Re-aim at the sun every 30–60 min.
- Can **pasteurize water** (WAPI indicator melts at 149°F/65°C).

## Dutch Oven Charcoal Guide
For **350°F**, using a 12-inch oven: about **17 briquettes on the lid + 8 underneath**.
Rule of thumb: **diameter (inches) × 2 = total coals**. Put ⅔ on top for baking, ⅓ bottom. Each extra briquette ≈ +10–15°F. Rotate the oven ¼ turn and lid ¼ turn opposite every 15 minutes.

*Your cast iron recipes ([Biscuits](/recipes/Buttermilk%20Biscuits) and [Peach Cobbler](/recipes/Peach%20Cobbler)) adapt well to a Dutch oven over coals.*

## Fuel Planning
- 1 lb propane cylinder ≈ 1.5–2 hrs on one burner at medium.
- 20 lb tank ≈ 20+ hrs of cooking for a two-burner stove used moderately.
- Store fuel **outdoors** or in a detached shed.
- Cook once, eat twice: big batches save fuel.

## Grain Mill
Wheat berries and dried corn last decades but need grinding. A **hand grain mill** (burr type) is essential if you store whole grains.
