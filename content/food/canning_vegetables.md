# Canning Vegetables

**Every plain vegetable is low-acid and must be pressure canned.** No exceptions: not green beans, not corn, not "just this once" in a water bath. Use the [Canning Lookup](/tools/canning) for your altitude and gauge, and read [Canning Safety](/food/canning_safety) for the full step-by-step.

## Processing Times (0–1,000 ft, 10 lb weighted / 11 lb dial)

[[canning:vegetables]]

*Above 1,000 ft: use 15 lb on a weighted gauge, or 12–15 lb on a dial gauge (see the Lookup tool). Times stay the same.*

## Hot Pack vs Raw Pack
- **Hot pack:** boil or simmer the food briefly, pack hot, cover with boiling cooking liquid. Shrinks the food first, so jars hold more, there's less floating, better color, and it's easier to remove air. **Preferred for most vegetables.**
- **Raw pack:** pack raw, cover with boiling water. Faster, but food shrinks in the jar and may float.
- Salt is optional (½ tsp per pint, 1 tsp per quart) for flavor only. It doesn't preserve anything.

## Prep Notes by Vegetable
| Vegetable | Prep |
|---|---|
| **Green beans** | Snap ends, cut 1–2 in pieces. Hot pack: boil 5 min. Raw pack tightly. |
| **Corn** | Blanch ears 3 min, cut kernels at ⅔ depth (don't scrape cob for whole kernel). Hot pack: add 1 cup boiling water per 4 cups corn, boil 5 min. |
| **Carrots** | Peel, slice or dice. Hot pack: simmer 5 min. |
| **Potatoes** | Peel, cube ½ in (or 1–2 in whole). Dip in ascorbic acid solution to prevent darkening, boil 2 min (cubes) / 10 min (whole), drain, cover with fresh boiling water. |
| **Sweet potatoes** | Boil or steam 15–20 min until partly soft, peel, cut. Cover with boiling water or light syrup. **Don't mash.** |
| **Beets** | Leave 1 in stem and taproot, boil 15–25 min until skins slip, peel, slice or cube. |
| **Dried beans** | Soak 12–18 hrs (or boil 2 min, soak 1 hr), drain, boil 30 min in fresh water, pack hot with liquid. Shelf-stable cooked beans ready to eat. |
| **Peas** | Shell, hot pack: boil 2 min. Wash well. |
| **Pumpkin / winter squash** | Peel, cube 1 in, boil 2 min, pack **cubes** in cooking liquid. Mash it when you open the jar, never before. |
| **Greens** | Wash thoroughly, steam 3–5 min until wilted, pack loosely. |
| **Okra** | Young pods, cook 2 min, pack hot. |
| **Peppers** | Remove seeds, blister skins (char and peel), flatten, pack loosely, cover with boiling water. Half-pints or pints only. |
| **Mixed vegetables** | Combine prepared vegetables, boil 5 min, pack hot. Process for the **longest** time of any ingredient (the table's 75/90 min covers common mixes). |

## Do NOT Can These (no safe tested method)
- **Mashed or puréed** pumpkin, winter squash, or sweet potatoes: too dense for heat to penetrate. Freeze or dry instead.
- **Cauliflower, broccoli, Brussels sprouts, cabbage** (plain): turn strong, mushy and discolored. Pickle or freeze them instead (pickled cauliflower is fine).
- **Summer squash / zucchini** (plain): no tested time. Pickle (relish) or freeze it.
- **Refried beans, mashed potatoes, purées** of any vegetable.
- **Anything thickened** with flour, cornstarch, rice, pasta, dairy, or eggs.
- Home-canned **garlic or herbs in oil**: botulism risk. Refrigerate up to 4 days or freeze.

## Yields (approx.)
| Fresh | Yields |
|---|---|
| Green beans, 1 bushel (30 lb) | 12–20 quarts (~2 lb per quart) |
| Corn, 1 bushel in husks (35 lb) | 6–11 quarts kernels |
| Carrots, 1 bushel without tops (50 lb) | 17–25 quarts |
| Potatoes, 1 bushel (50 lb) | 18–22 quarts |
| Peas in pod, 1 bushel (30 lb) | 5–10 quarts |
| Beets, 1 bushel without tops (52 lb) | 15–20 quarts |

## Priority
- **Vegetables = pressure canner. Cube, don't mash.**
