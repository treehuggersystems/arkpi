# Prayer & Family Devotions

[[Philippians 4:6-7]]

## The Lord's Prayer: Jesus' Model

[[Matthew 6:9-13]]

Use it as an outline: worship God's name → pray for His kingdom and will → ask for daily needs → confess and forgive → ask for protection from temptation and evil.

## A.C.T.S.: A Simple Prayer Pattern
- **A**doration: praise God for who He is (faithful, powerful, good, near)
- **C**onfession: honestly name your sins and receive forgiveness (1 John 1:9)
- **T**hanksgiving: thank Him for specific things, even small ones (1 Thessalonians 5:18)
- **S**upplication: ask for your needs and pray for others by name

## Praying When You're Afraid
- Tell God exactly what you're afraid of. He already knows, and He invites it (1 Peter 5:7).
- Pray Scripture back to Him: "*You said You will never leave me nor forsake me…*" (Hebrews 13:5).
- Pray for **others** as well. It lifts your eyes off yourself.
- Short "arrow prayers" through the day: "*Lord, help.*" "*Give me wisdom.*" "*Thank You.*"

## 15-Minute Family Devotion
Do it daily, after supper or before bed by lantern light. Consistency matters more than length.
1. **Sing** one hymn or chorus (2 min).
2. **Read** a short passage: a psalm, a chapter of a Gospel, or a few Proverbs (3 min). Use the [reading plan](/bible/plan).
3. **Talk** (5 min):
   - What does this teach us about God?
   - What does it teach about us?
   - How can we live it out tomorrow?
4. **Pray** (5 min): everyone prays, even a sentence. Pray for neighbors, the sick, leaders, and those without Christ.

**For young children:** Bible stories (Genesis, Exodus, Daniel, Jonah, the Gospels), one memory verse a week, and let them pray first.

## Fasting
Jesus said "*when* ye fast" (Matthew 6:16-18). Fasting is setting aside food for a time to focus on prayer and dependence on God.
- In a food shortage you may already be eating less. Offer that to God in prayer.
- Medical caution: people with diabetes, pregnancy, or health conditions should not fast from food. Fast from something else (media, a meal, a comfort).

## Keep a Prayer Journal
Write requests with dates, then write how God answered. In hard seasons, reading back over God's faithfulness is powerful (Psalm 77:11).

## Praying for Your Community
- Local, state and national leaders (1 Timothy 2:1-2)
- Doctors, nurses, first responders and line workers
- Your pastor and church members
- Neighbors by name, especially the elderly, sick, and those who don't know Christ
- Wisdom for your family's decisions
