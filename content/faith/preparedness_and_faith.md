# Preparedness & Faith — Is Prepping Biblical?

## Scripture Commends Wise Preparation

[[Hebrews 11:7]]

[[Proverbs 22:3]]

[[Proverbs 6:6-8]]

[[Proverbs 21:20]]

[[1 Timothy 5:8]]

- **Joseph** stored a fifth of Egypt's harvest for seven years and saved nations from famine (Genesis 41:33-36, 47-49).
- **Noah** prepared an ark "to the saving of his house" by faith, at God's warning. That's where this ARK gets its name.
- **The early church** sent relief ahead of a prophesied famine (Acts 11:27-30).

## But Our Trust Is in God, Not in Our Stockpile

[[Matthew 6:31-34]]

[[Psalm 20:7]]

[[Luke 12:16-21]]

[[Psalm 127:1]]

The danger isn't storing food. It's **trusting** the storage, hoarding in fear, or growing proud of our self-sufficiency (James 4:13-15). Prepare with open hands.

## Prepare to Give, Not Just to Survive
In a crisis, a prepared Christian household can be a **lighthouse** for its neighborhood.

[[Galatians 6:10]]

[[Acts 2:44-45]]

[[Luke 10:33-34]]

[[2 Corinthians 9:7-8]]

Practical ideas:
- **Store extra** specifically to share: rice, beans, water filters, blankets, Bibles and tracts.
- Know your neighbors **now**: the widow down the street, the family with the new baby, the man on oxygen.
- Plan with your church to pool and distribute resources fairly.
- Generosity and wisdom can work together. You can help others discreetly without endangering your family.

## Honesty, Order and Neighbor-Love When Systems Fail
- **Honest dealing** in barter and trade (Proverbs 11:1, Leviticus 19:35-36).
- **Respect lawful authority** (Romans 13:1-7), while obeying God first when the two conflict (Acts 5:29).
- **Don't take advantage** of anyone's desperation (Proverbs 22:22-23).
- **Pursue peace** as far as it depends on you (Romans 12:18).
- **Protect the vulnerable** (Psalm 82:3-4, Proverbs 24:11).

## A Prepper's Prayer
> *Lord, everything we have comes from You. Give us wisdom to prepare like Joseph, faith to trust You like Noah, and open hands to share like the Good Samaritan. Keep us from fear and from pride. If hard days come, make our home a place of refuge and our lives a witness to Jesus. Amen.*
