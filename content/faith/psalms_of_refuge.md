## How to Pray the Psalms
- **Read slowly**, out loud if you can. They were written to be sung and spoken.
- **Make it personal:** "The LORD is *my* shepherd" means you. Put your family's names in the verses.
- **Be honest.** The psalms include fear, anger, and confusion (see Psalm 13, Psalm 42, Psalm 88). God can handle your honest prayers.
- **End in trust.** Most lament psalms turn from "How long, O LORD?" to "But I have trusted in thy mercy" (Psalm 13:5).

More psalms for hard nights: Psalm 4 (sleep), Psalm 16, Psalm 31, Psalm 34, Psalm 37 (don't fret), Psalm 62, Psalm 103 (praise), Psalm 139 (God knows you), Psalm 142.
