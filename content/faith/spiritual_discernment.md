# Spiritual Discernment in a Crisis

When the world is shaken, people become spiritually hungry and also **vulnerable**. History shows that disasters, wars and pandemics bring a flood of false prophets, new "revelations," charismatic leaders demanding loyalty, fear-driven end-times speculation, and a surge of interest in the occult. Jesus warned us ahead of time so we wouldn't be caught off guard.

[[Matthew 24:4-5]]

[[Matthew 24:24]]

## Test Everything Against Scripture

[[1 John 4:1]]

[[Acts 17:11]]

[[Galatians 1:8]]

The Bible is our **final authority**: complete, sufficient, and true (2 Timothy 3:16-17). Any message, vision, dream, or prophecy that adds to it, contradicts it, or replaces it is to be rejected, no matter how impressive the messenger or how dramatic the signs (Deuteronomy 13:1-4).

## Red Flags of False Teachers & Cults
- **New revelation** that supersedes or "unlocks" the Bible
- **A leader** who claims unique authority, special access to God, or that *only* his group will be saved
- **Isolation:** cut off family, other churches, outside information
- **Control:** of money, food, marriages, movement, or what you may read
- **Date-setting** for Christ's return or for the end of the world
- **A different Jesus or a different gospel**: salvation by works, rituals, or loyalty to the group instead of by grace through faith (2 Corinthians 11:3-4)
- **Fear and pressure** as the main tools, with no room for questions
- **Greed:** "give to receive," selling blessings or protection (2 Peter 2:1-3)
- **Moral double standards** for the leader (Matthew 7:15-20: "by their fruits ye shall know them")

[[2 Peter 2:1]]

## The Occult and Spiritual Counterfeits
In desperate times, people turn to fortune-telling, mediums, spirit contact, charms, astrology, and "energy" practices for answers and protection. Scripture is clear: these are forbidden and dangerous.

[[Deuteronomy 18:10-12]]

Lovingly steer people instead to the God who actually answers prayer.

## About the End Times
Christians, Baptists included, hold different views about the order and timing of end-times events. Hold firmly to what Scripture plainly teaches, and don't let speculation divide believers or drive fear:

- **Jesus Christ will return** personally, bodily, and visibly (Acts 1:11).
- **No one knows the day or hour** (Matthew 24:36). Anyone who claims to know is wrong.
- We are called to be **watchful and faithful**, not panicked (Matthew 24:42-46).
- Hard times are not proof of a particular date. They're a call to readiness, repentance, and evangelism.
- Our hope is not in escaping trouble but in **Christ Himself**.

[[Matthew 24:36]]

[[1 Thessalonians 5:1-6]]

## Practical Safeguards
- **Stay rooted in a local body** of believers with accountable leaders.
- **Read whole books of the Bible**, not just favorite verses out of context.
- **Know the core truths of the faith**: the Trinity, the deity and humanity of Christ, salvation by grace through faith, the authority of Scripture, the bodily resurrection.
- **Pray for wisdom** (James 1:5).
- **Be wary of anyone** who suddenly gains followers during a crisis by claiming special knowledge.
- **Don't let fear make your decisions.** God has not given us a spirit of fear (2 Timothy 1:7).

[[James 1:5]]

## Priority
- **Scripture is the test. Christ is the center. Fear is not your guide.**
