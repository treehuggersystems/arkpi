# The Gospel — How to Be Saved

In a crisis, people start asking the biggest questions. *What happens when I die? Can God forgive me?* This is the good news, laid out simply. Share it.

## The Romans Road

### 1. Everyone has sinned

[[Romans 3:23]]

We've all broken God's law, in thought, word and deed. Being "good enough" isn't possible.

### 2. Sin brings death, but God offers a gift

[[Romans 6:23]]

The penalty for sin is death: separation from God forever. But eternal life is a **gift**. It isn't earned.

### 3. Christ died for us

[[Romans 5:8]]

Jesus, God the Son, lived a sinless life and died on the cross in our place, paying sin's penalty. He rose from the dead on the third day (1 Corinthians 15:3-4).

### 4. Believe and confess

[[Romans 10:9-10]]

[[Romans 10:13]]

### 5. Saved by grace, through faith

[[Ephesians 2:8-9]]

Salvation is not by baptism, church membership, or good works. It's by **grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone**. Good works follow as the fruit of a changed life (Ephesians 2:10).

### 6. Peace with God and assurance

[[Romans 5:1]]

[[1 John 5:13]]

## Responding to Christ
There's no magic formula. God looks at the heart. A person who turns from sin and trusts Jesus might pray something like:

> *"Lord Jesus, I know I'm a sinner and I can't save myself. I believe You died for my sins and rose from the dead. I turn from my sin and put my trust in You alone. Come into my life and be my Lord and Savior. Thank You for forgiving me and giving me eternal life. Amen."*

## Next Steps for a New Believer
1. **Tell someone.** (Romans 10:9)
2. **Be baptized** by immersion as a public testimony of your faith (Matthew 28:19, Acts 8:36-38, Romans 6:4). Baptism doesn't save; it shows you've been saved.
3. **Read the Bible daily.** Start with the Gospel of John.
4. **Pray**, talking with God about everything (see [Prayer & Devotions](/faith/prayer_and_devotions)).
5. **Gather with other believers** (Hebrews 10:24-25), even if it's just a few families in a living room.

## Sharing the Gospel in a Crisis
- Meet physical needs with compassion. Love opens hearts (James 2:15-16).
- Listen first. Ask: "*How are you holding up? Do you have any spiritual beliefs that help you through this?*"
- Use Scripture. Mark the Romans Road in a pocket New Testament.
- Be gentle and respectful (1 Peter 3:15). Don't exploit fear.
- Leave the results to God. Our job is to plant and water (1 Corinthians 3:6-7).
