# Church Without a Building

The early church met in homes (Acts 2:46, Romans 16:5). If roads close, power fails, or gathering becomes difficult, the church doesn't stop. **The church is the people, not the building.**

[[Hebrews 10:24-25]]

[[Acts 2:42]]

## Before Anything Happens
- Talk with your **pastor and deacons now**. Ask what the plan is if the church can't meet. Who leads small gatherings, how members check on each other, and how the ordinances will be handled.
- Keep a **printed church directory** with addresses, and divide it into **neighborhood groups** with a deacon or trusted member responsible for each.
- Agree on a meeting place and time (e.g. *Sundays 10:00 at the Smiths'*), and add it to your [PACE plan](/comms/pace_comms_plan).
- Keep printed Bibles, a hymnal or printed hymns (see [Hymns for Hard Times](/faith/hymns_for_hard_times)), and this ARK on hand.

## A Simple Order of Worship
1. **Welcome & call to worship.** Read a psalm (e.g. Psalm 100 or Psalm 46).
2. **Hymn.** Sing together, without instruments if necessary.
3. **Prayer.** Praise, confession, thanksgiving, and requests for the sick, the grieving, and those in need.
4. **Scripture reading.** Read a full passage aloud.
5. **Teaching.** A pastor, deacon, or a man of good testimony the group trusts (1 Timothy 3; Titus 1) explains the passage and applies it. Simple faithfulness beats eloquence.
6. **Hymn.**
7. **Sharing needs.** Who needs food, medicine, firewood, a visit? Organize help *right then*.
8. **The Lord's Supper** (when observed; see below).
9. **Benediction.** For example Numbers 6:24-26.

[[Numbers 6:24-26]]

## The Lord's Supper
Baptists observe the Lord's Supper as a **memorial ordinance**: a remembrance of Christ's death and a proclamation of it until He comes. It's for baptized believers, preceded by self-examination. Practice varies between Baptist churches (who presides, how often, whether it is observed outside the gathered congregation), so **follow your church's convictions** and settle this with your pastor ahead of time.

[[1 Corinthians 11:23-26]]

- **Elements:** unleavened bread or crackers; grape juice (or what's available).
- **Order:** read the passage, pray and give thanks for the bread, eat together; pray and give thanks for the cup, drink together; close with a hymn (Matthew 26:30).
- Invite a time of silent **self-examination and confession** first (1 Corinthians 11:28).

## Baptism
Baptists hold to **believer's baptism by immersion**, following a credible profession of faith, as a picture of death to sin and new life in Christ (Romans 6:3-4). Baptism is an act of obedience, **not a requirement for salvation** (Luke 23:42-43).
- A creek, pond, lake, or stock tank works, as it did in the New Testament (Acts 8:36-38).
- The candidate gives a public testimony of faith in Christ before witnesses.
- "*In obedience to the command of our Lord, and upon your profession of faith, I baptize you, my brother/sister, in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.*" (Matthew 28:19)
- Safety first: calm, shallow water, two people assisting, check for currents and cold.

## Caring for One Another
This is when the church shines brightest (Acts 4:32-35).
- **Deacons organize care:** who's elderly, sick, alone, out of food, out of medication?
- **Benevolence pantry:** members contribute what they can; distribute by need.
- **Visit** shut-ins on foot or by bike. Bring Scripture and a hymn as well as food.
- **Widows, orphans, the vulnerable** are a biblical priority (James 1:27).
- **Neighbors who aren't believers** too (Galatians 6:10). Love is the strongest testimony.

## Keeping Teaching Sound
Without a pastor for a while, groups can drift. Stay in Scripture, read whole books of the Bible together, and test any new teaching against God's Word (Acts 17:11). See [Spiritual Discernment](/faith/spiritual_discernment).
