# Cold Press Soap

## Ingredients
- Lye
- Water
- Oils (olive, coconut)

## Steps
1. Mix lye and water
2. Heat oils
3. Combine at correct temp
4. Pour into mold

## Notes
- Always add lye to water, not water to lye
- Wear gloves
