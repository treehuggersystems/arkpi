# Lye Safety

## What is Lye?
- Sodium hydroxide (NaOH)
- Highly caustic

---

## Critical Rule
- ALWAYS add lye to water  
- NEVER add water to lye

---

## PPE (Required)
- Gloves
- Eye protection
- Long sleeves

---

## Mixing
- Do it in a well-ventilated area
- Avoid inhaling fumes

---

## Burns
- Rinse immediately with water
- Seek medical help for severe exposure

---

## Storage
- Keep dry
- Keep sealed
- Keep away from children

---

## Priority
- Treat lye like a serious chemical—because it is
