# Sanitation & Hygiene When Systems Fail

After disasters, **disease from poor sanitation often kills more people than the disaster itself.** Human waste must stay away from water, food, hands and flies.

## Two-Bucket Toilet (best for homes/apartments)
Separating urine from feces cuts odor dramatically.

**Pee bucket:**
- Urine is nearly sterile when fresh. Dilute ~10:1 with water and pour on non-edible plants/lawn, or dispose in a dedicated pit away from water.

**Poo bucket (5-gal + toilet seat lid):**
1. Line with a heavy-duty trash bag (double bag).
2. Add a layer of cover material in the bottom.
3. After each use, cover completely with **sawdust, wood shavings, peat moss, coco coir, shredded leaves, or cat litter**. No smell, no flies.
4. Toilet paper goes in the bucket.
5. When ⅔ full: tie off, store in a sealed container away from the house, or bury (see latrine rules).

**Stock:** 2–3 buckets with lids, toilet seat lid, contractor bags, a few bags of cover material, TP, gloves, bleach.

## Outdoor Latrine
- At least **200 ft (70 big steps) from any water source**, well, stream, or garden, and **downhill** from water.
- **Cathole** (single use, outdoors): 6–8 in deep, 4–6 in wide; cover and disguise.
- **Trench latrine** (group): 1 ft wide, 2–3 ft deep, as long as needed. Cover after each use with a shovel of soil. Fill when within 1 ft of the top; mound soil over it.
- Keep a **lid** to block flies. Put the **handwash station** at the exit.

## Handwashing
- **Soap + water, 20 seconds**, scrubbing fronts, backs, between fingers, under nails. Rinse in running water.
- **Tippy tap:** a jug hung on a stick, tipped with a foot pedal rope. Hands-free, uses very little water.
- Hand sanitizer (≥ 60% alcohol) works when hands aren't visibly dirty.
- **Wash:** after the toilet, before cooking/eating, after handling waste/trash/animals, after caring for the sick.

## Disinfecting Surfaces
- **General disinfectant:** about **⅓ cup unscented bleach per gallon of water** (CDC: 5 Tbsp per gallon). Mix fresh daily. Never mix with ammonia or vinegar (toxic gas).
- Food-prep surfaces: wash first, then sanitize with **1 Tbsp bleach per gallon**, air dry.

## Bathing With Little Water
- Sponge bath: a basin of warm water, washcloth. Face → body → feet → groin last.
- Prioritize: hands, face, armpits, groin, feet daily.
- Baby wipes / no-rinse body wash for when water is very limited.
- Dry shampoo, cornstarch.

## Dental
- Brush twice daily. Use purified water. Baking soda works as toothpaste.
- Floss. Dental emergencies are miserable without a dentist.

## Trash
- Separate: food waste (compost or bury deeply away from water, or burn), recyclables, burnables, and hazardous waste.
- Seal food waste to avoid rats and flies. Burn paper/cardboard if allowed and safe.
- Never burn plastics or treated materials near people.

## Greywater (washing water)
- Reuse for **flushing toilets** or watering ornamental plants.
- Don't let it pool (mosquitoes). Dispose away from wells.

## Pests
Flies and rats spread disease. Cover food and waste, keep a clean camp/home, screen windows, and set traps.

## Women's Hygiene
Stock pads/tampons or reusable options (menstrual cups, washable pads) for months. Reusables are the long-term answer.
