# Fire Starting Basics

## Fire Triangle
You need:
- Heat
- Fuel
- Oxygen

---

## Tinder
- Dry leaves
- Cotton balls
- Dryer lint
- Birch bark

---

## Kindling
- Small sticks
- Pencil-size wood

---

## Fuel
- Larger logs

---

## Methods

### Lighter / Matches
- Most reliable
- Always carry backup

---

### Ferro Rod
- Works when wet
- Requires good tinder

---

### Battery + Steel Wool
- Sparks instantly
- Short-term emergency use

---

## Tips
- Build structure BEFORE lighting
- Protect from wind
- Start small, grow gradually

---

## Priority
- Dry tinder is EVERYTHING
