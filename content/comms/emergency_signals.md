# Emergency Signals (Ground-to-Air, Visual, Audible)

## The Rule of Three
**Three of anything** signals distress: 3 fires in a triangle, 3 whistle blasts, 3 flashes, 3 piles of rocks, 3 gunshots.

## Ground-to-Air Symbols (ICAO / FAA)

Make them **at least 10 ft (3 m) tall** with high contrast — logs on snow, rocks on grass, trenches in sand, bright cloth.

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **V** | Require assistance |
| **X** | Require **medical** assistance |
| **N** | No / negative |
| **Y** | Yes / affirmative |
| **→** (arrow) | Proceeding in this direction |
| **SOS** | Distress |

## Aircraft Replies

| Aircraft does | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Rocks wings side to side (day) / flashes green (night) | Message **received and understood** |
| Makes a complete right-hand circle (day) / flashes red (night) | Message received but **not understood** |

## Body Signals

| Signal | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Both arms raised overhead in a **Y** | **Yes / I need help — land here** |
| One arm up, one arm down diagonally (**N**) | No / don't need help |
| Lying flat on back, arms overhead | Need medical assistance |

## Signal Mirror
1. Hold the mirror near your eye; extend your other arm toward the aircraft with fingers in a **V**.
2. Tilt the mirror so the light spot falls on your fingers, then put the target between your fingers.
3. Wiggle slightly to flash. Mirror flashes can be seen **10+ miles** away.
4. Stop flashing once they've seen you — don't blind the pilot.

No mirror? Use a phone screen, CD, polished metal, or a car mirror.

## Fire & Smoke
- **Daytime:** smoke. Add **green leaves, moss, or rubber** for thick white/black smoke against the background.
- **Night:** bright flame. Build three fires in a triangle, ~100 ft apart.
- Prepare the signal fires in advance; light them when you hear aircraft.

## Whistle
- **3 blasts** = help. **2 blasts** = come here / where are you. **1 blast** = where are you / OK.
- A whistle carries far further than your voice and uses almost no energy.

## Lights
- Flashlight / strobe: **SOS** or **3 flashes** at intervals.
- Car headlights / hazards, and honk the horn in threes.
- Chemlights swung on a cord make a large visible circle at night.

## Make Yourself Big
- Get to **open ground** — clearings, ridgelines, riverbanks.
- Spread out bright gear; stay put if people know your plan.
- Searchers look for **straight lines and right angles** — nature doesn't make them.
