## Numbers

| Digit | Say | Digit | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | ZE-ro | 5 | FIFE |
| 1 | WUN | 6 | SIX |
| 2 | TOO | 7 | SEV-en |
| 3 | TREE | 8 | AIT |
| 4 | FOW-er | 9 | NIN-er |

- Say numbers **digit by digit**: 146.52 → "*one four six decimal five two*"
- Grid / coordinates: "*grid: one six Sierra, Golf Juliett, one two three four five, six seven eight nine zero*"

## Procedure Words (Prowords)

| Proword | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **MAYDAY** ×3 | Life-threatening emergency — all other traffic stops |
| **PAN-PAN** ×3 | Urgent but not immediately life-threatening |
| **SÉCURITÉ** ×3 | Safety message (hazard, weather) |
| BREAK | Pause / separating parts of a message |
| BREAK-BREAK | Interrupting with priority traffic |
| OVER | My transmission is done, I expect a reply |
| OUT | Conversation finished, no reply expected (never "over and out") |
| ROGER | I received your last transmission |
| WILCO | I understand and **will comply** (includes Roger) |
| AFFIRMATIVE / NEGATIVE | Yes / No |
| SAY AGAIN | Repeat (never say "repeat" — it means fire again in artillery) |
| I SPELL | I'm about to spell phonetically |
| FIGURES | Numbers follow |
| WAIT / WAIT OUT | Short pause / longer pause, I'll call back |
| CORRECTION | I made an error; correct version follows |
| READ BACK | Repeat my message back to me |
| RADIO CHECK | How do you hear me? |
| LOUD AND CLEAR / WEAK / BROKEN | Signal report |

## How to Make a Call

1. **Listen first.** Make sure the channel is clear.
2. "*[Their call sign/name], this is [yours], over.*"
3. Wait. Repeat once or twice, then stop and try later.
4. When answered: say your message in short chunks; say **OVER** after each.
5. End with "*[your call sign], OUT.*" — hams must ID every 10 minutes and at the end.

## Distress Call (Voice)

> **MAYDAY, MAYDAY, MAYDAY**
> This is *[name / call sign]* ×3
> **MAYDAY** *[name / call sign]*
> My location is *[grid, address, landmarks]*
> Nature of emergency: *[injury, fire, trapped…]*
> Assistance needed: *[medical, evacuation…]*
> Number of people: *[n]*, *[injuries]*
> I will listen on *[frequency]*. **OVER**

Then **listen**. Repeat at intervals.
