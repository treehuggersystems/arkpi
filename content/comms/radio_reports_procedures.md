# Radio Reports & Net Procedures

Standard formats make messages short, complete, and easy to write down. Log everything in the [Logbook](/tools/logbook).

## SALUTE — Observation Report

| Line | Content |
|---|---|
| **S**ize | How many people / vehicles |
| **A**ctivity | What they're doing |
| **L**ocation | Grid / address / landmark |
| **U**nit / uniform | Description, clothing, markings |
| **T**ime | When observed |
| **E**quipment | What they carry / drive |

## SITREP — Situation Report

1. **Who:** reporting station
2. **Status:** OK / need help (green / amber / red)
3. **People:** number, injuries
4. **Supplies:** water, food, fuel, power — days remaining
5. **Infrastructure:** roads, power, water, comms in your area
6. **Needs / requests**
7. **Next check-in time**

## Medical Evacuation Request (adapted 9-line)

| Line | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Location of pickup |
| 2 | Frequency / call sign at pickup |
| 3 | Number of patients by urgency (urgent / priority / routine) |
| 4 | Special equipment needed (litter, oxygen, extraction) |
| 5 | Number of patients: litter vs walking |
| 6 | Security at pickup site |
| 7 | How the site is marked (panel, smoke color, light) |
| 8 | Patient details (age, nature of injuries) |
| 9 | Terrain / obstacles at the site |

## METHANE — Major Incident Report

**M**ajor incident declared · **E**xact location · **T**ype of incident · **H**azards · **A**ccess routes · **N**umber of casualties · **E**mergency services present / needed

## Running a Neighborhood Net

A **net** is a scheduled, organized on-air meeting. One person is **Net Control (NCS)**.

1. NCS opens: "*This is [call], net control for the [name] net. This is a directed net. Stations with emergency traffic call now.*"
2. Take emergency/priority traffic first.
3. Roll call or open check-ins: stations give call sign + "no traffic" or "with traffic."
4. NCS directs each exchange: "*[Station A], go ahead with your traffic for [Station B].*"
5. Close: "*This net is closed at [time]. Next session [time]. [call] clear.*"

**Rules:** speak only when directed, keep transmissions short, write everything down.

## Radio Discipline
- **Think, press, pause half a second, then speak.** Release when done.
- Keep transmissions under **30 seconds**; break long messages.
- Don't broadcast exact supply amounts, home addresses or travel plans in the clear when security matters — use code words from your [PACE plan](/comms/pace_comms_plan).
- Assume **everyone** can hear you.

## Battery Conservation
- Use **low power** for close contacts.
- Scan only during agreed windows.
- Turn off backlights, keypad beeps, and VOX.
- Carry an **AA battery pack** for your handheld — AAs are easier to find than proprietary packs.
- Cold kills battery capacity: keep spares inside your jacket.
