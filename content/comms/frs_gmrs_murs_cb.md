# FRS, GMRS, MURS & CB Channel Charts

## FRS / GMRS (UHF)

FRS radios are license-free. GMRS uses the same channels at higher power and adds repeaters — one $35 license (no test) covers you and your immediate family for 10 years.

**"Privacy codes" (CTCSS/DCS) are not private** — they only filter what *you* hear. Anyone can listen. Turn codes **off** when monitoring for help.

### Realistic range
- Handheld to handheld, in town: **0.5–2 miles**
- Open terrain / hilltop: 3–5+ miles
- GMRS mobile with roof antenna: 5–15 miles; via repeater: 20–50+ miles

## CB (27 MHz HF, license-free)

Limits: **4 W** AM/FM, **12 W PEP** SSB (channels used for SSB by convention: 36–40). Skip (long-distance bounce) is common when the sun is active.

## Choosing a Service

| Need | Best choice |
|---|---|
| Family on the same property / block | FRS |
| Neighborhood, across town | GMRS (mobile/base + repeater) |
| Rural acreage, outdoors | MURS or GMRS |
| Talking to travelers / truckers | CB |
| Regional / statewide | Ham HF (40 m / 80 m) |
