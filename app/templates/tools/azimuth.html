Reading the result

  • True azimuth is from true north, for maps and the stars.
  • Magnetic is what to set on a compass.
  • Back azimuth is the bearing for the trip home.
  • Mils: 6400 to a circle (military compasses).
  • Line of sight assumes radio refraction (4/3 Earth). Trees and buildings aren't in the terrain data.
