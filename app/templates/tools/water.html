Rules of thumb

  • 1 gallon per person per day is the FEMA / Red Cross minimum (about ½ gal drinking, ½ gal food prep & hygiene). Store at least 3 days; aim for 2 weeks.
  • Children, nursing mothers, sick people and hot weather all need more. Heat can double requirements.
  • Pets: roughly 1 oz of water per lb of body weight per day.
  • Water weighs 8.34 lb per gallon. A full 55-gal drum is about 460 lb, so place it before filling.
  • Your water heater (30–80 gal) and toilet tanks (not bowls) are emergency reserves.
