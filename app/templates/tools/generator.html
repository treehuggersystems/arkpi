Carbon monoxide kills

Never run a generator indoors, in a garage (even with the door open), or near windows. Keep it 20+ ft from the house with the exhaust pointed away. Run battery CO alarms inside.

Stretch your fuel

  • Run in blocks (e.g. 2 hrs morning and evening) to cool the fridge/freezer and charge batteries, not 24/7. A full freezer holds ~48 hrs closed; a fridge ~4 hrs.
  • Generators are least efficient at light load. Batch your loads while it runs.
  • Gasoline: treat with stabilizer. Untreated it lasts ~3–6 months; stabilized ~1–2 years. Rotate into vehicles.
  • Propane doesn't degrade, which makes it the best long-term storage fuel. A 20 lb tank holds ~4.7 gal.
  • Estimate model: burn ≈ spec × (0.4 + 1.2 × load fraction). Real-world use varies ±25%.
