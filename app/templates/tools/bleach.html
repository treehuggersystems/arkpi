Use only plain, unscented bleach

No scented, "splash-less", color-safe or cleaner-added bleach. Bleach loses strength over time; bottles older than about a year may be weak. Bleach does not remove chemicals, fuel or heavy metals, and it is weak against Cryptosporidium. Filter or boil when you can.

Procedure (EPA)

  1. If the water is cloudy, let it settle and filter it through clean cloth, a paper towel or a coffee filter first.
  2. Add the bleach dose above and stir or shake well.
  3. Let stand 30 minutes. It should have a slight chlorine smell.
  4. No smell? Repeat the dose and wait another 15 minutes. If it still doesn't smell of chlorine, discard it and find another source.
